Stericycle is a Fortune 500 compliance company specializing in regulated medical waste disposal, sharps management, pharmaceutical waste, secure information destruction (Shred-it), and healthcare compliance training (Steri-Safe). In November 2024, Stericycle was acquired by Waste Management (WM) for approximately $7.2 billion and now operates as WM Healthcare Solutions division.
